# Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar

This office lists **14 services for residents** in the Municipality of Tago's Citizen's Charter.

## Where to go

Tago Municipal Hall, Purisima, Tago, Surigao del Sur.

**This project has not confirmed which room or floor this office occupies**, and the municipality does not publish a floor plan. Ask at the hall.

## Office hours

**Not stated.** Checked on 9 August 2026 against the municipality's website and against this office's own Citizen's Charter; neither states them.

Office hours are the single most useful thing missing from this directory, and this page will keep saying so rather than printing a likely answer. National office hours are not this office's hours, and last year's are not this year's.

## How to reach it

The municipality publishes one landline and one email address for the municipal hall as a whole, on its own contact page. **No separate number or address is published for this office.**

This project records no personal mobile number and no personal email address for anyone — an institutional channel or nothing.

## Who heads it

**Recorded by role, not by name.** The head of this office is its office head; where the municipality publishes a name, it is published on the municipality's own site and that is the place to read it.

This is a deliberate choice rather than a gap. A name goes out of date on an appointment or an election, and a stale name on a civic page is worse than no name — it sends someone to ask for a person who has moved on.

## What it does

The municipality publishes a page for this office at [https://tago.gov.ph/municipal-offices/executive-offices/municipal-civil-registrar/](https://tago.gov.ph/municipal-offices/executive-offices/municipal-civil-registrar/), read on 2026-08-09. **It carries no statement of the office's mandate** — no page under the municipality's office directory does.

So there is no mandate quoted here. What this office does is described below by what it actually provides, taken from its own Citizen's Charter — which is the municipality's own account of its work, and is more use to a resident than a mandate statement would be.

Each is listed in this office's Citizen's Charter. **The requirements, fees and processing times are in the charter document itself, linked below** — this project publishes that the service exists and who provides it, and does not restate the document's contents.

- Register a birth
- Register a marriage
- Register a death that happened in Tago
- Register a death
- Apply for a marriage license
- Request an electronic endorsement of a civil registry record
- Request an electronic endorsement with certified copies
- Have a child legitimated after the parents marry
- Use the father’s surname on a birth record
- Add missing details to a civil registry record
- Change a first name on a civil registry record
- Correct a clerical error on a civil registry record
- Correct the sex or birth date on a birth record
- Get a PSA copy of a birth, marriage or death certificate

## What the charter calls this office

The charter names this office **10 different ways**. Each is reproduced exactly as published, because the counter will use its own wording and a resident matching a page to a form needs the published words:

- `Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar`
- `OFFICE OF THE MUNICIPAL CIVIL REGISTRAR` — Published in capitals.
- `Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar - Registration Division`
- `OFFICE OF THE MUNICIPAL CIVIL REGISTRAR-REGISTRATION DIVISION` — Published in capitals, and without the spaces around the hyphen.
- `Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar - Regulatory Division`
- `Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar-Regulatory Division` — Differs from the row above only by the spaces around the hyphen.
- `Office of the Local Civil Registrar-Registration Division` — The document says “Local”.
- `Office of the City Civil Registrar-Regulatory Division` — The document says “City”. Tago is a municipality. Recorded as published; not corrected here.
- `Office of the City Municipal Civil Registrar - Registration Division` — The document says “City Municipal”, carrying both. Recorded as published; not corrected here.
- `Office of the Municipal` — The published string stops mid-name. Read from the document it appears in, which is the Civil Registrar’s.

**The charter is not silently corrected here.** Where its wording differs from the municipality's own status or from another entry, the difference is recorded rather than tidied away.

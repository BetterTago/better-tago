# Where ordinances and session records are published

**Who provides it:** Office of the Sangguniang Bayan, Tago Municipal Hall.

If you are looking for the text of a municipal ordinance, the list of who sits on which committee, or when the
council next meets — **this page is here to tell you that none of those is published online**, so you do not
spend an afternoon looking for something that is not there.

## What was checked, and when

On **9 August 2026** this project read, and dated:

- both Legislative office pages on the municipal website — the Sangguniang Bayan's and the Vice Mayor's;
- both Legislative Citizen's Charter documents;
- **every URL in the municipality's own sitemap** — 21 posts and 65 pages on the day of the check, including
  the ones filed as news. Not one is an ordinance, a resolution, a register, a session agenda or minutes, or a
  committee list.

The pages carrying people's names were counted and not recorded here. A name belongs beside a source and a
check date in this project's data layer, where one edit corrects it after an election — not copied into prose.

## What is published

**The Sangguniang Bayan's charter names one service a resident can ask for**, and the Vice Mayor's names one.
Both have a page here, and both link the document they came from.

## What is not

- **No ordinances or resolutions.** There is no register, no index, and no page listing them by number, title,
  date or subject.
- **No committee membership.** The council's composition is not published by position or committee.
- **No session schedule.** Nothing states when the council sits.

**This project has not filled any of those in, and will not from an unofficial source.** A committee list
assembled from news posts, or an ordinance summarised from a photograph, would be this project inventing a
public record — which is the opposite of what it is for.

## What would change this

Any of the three being published by the municipality, or supplied to this project in writing. If you know of an
official source that carries one, the correction link in the footer is the fastest way to say so — that is a
correction this project would be glad to make.

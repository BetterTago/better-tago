# Green Falls Mountain Liesure Farm & Resort

**Published as:** “GREEN FALLS MOUNTAIN LIESURE FARM & RESORT” on the municipality’s tourism page.

**Where it is:** Barangay Cabangahan, Tago.

**What it is:** A waterfall in a wooded setting.

## What the tourism page leaves unclear

**The heading and the description name different places.** The heading reads _GREEN FALLS MOUNTAIN LIESURE FARM
& RESORT_; the text beneath it describes _Cagpangi Falls_, in Barangay Cabangahan. Whether these are one place
under two names, or a resort and a waterfall listed together, is **not stated**.

Both are recorded as published. Choosing one would be resolving something the source does not resolve, and the
spelling _LIESURE_ is reproduced as the municipality prints it rather than corrected.

## What the tourism page does not state

Cost, season, safety information, opening hours, and how to reach it are **not stated** for this entry. They
are recorded as unknown rather than filled in from elsewhere: an entrance cost or a travel time that turns out
to be wrong costs somebody a wasted journey, and no source this project can cite states them. Checked 2026-08-09.

## Why the page's own wording is not restated here

The municipality's tourism page describes each place in promotional terms. **This project lists what exists and
where; it does not recommend, rate or rank a place or a business.** The page is linked below so a reader can
read the municipality's own description in the municipality's own words.

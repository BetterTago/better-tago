# What you can do here

**Ten services have a page so far.** Each one names the office that provides it and links the municipality's
own document, so you can see what to bring and what it costs before you travel.

## Register something, or get a copy

- Register a birth
- Register a marriage
- Get a PSA copy of a birth, marriage or death certificate

## Deal with a business

- Apply for a new business permit
- Renew a business permit

## See someone about your health

- See a doctor for a consultation
- Get a child immunized
- Get a medical certificate

## Get help

- Get a certificate of indigency
- Get support as a solo parent, senior citizen or person with disability

## The rest of the charter is not here yet

The municipality publishes **99 services for residents** across its Citizen's Charter. The ten above are the
ones most people need in a year, and they are the first to get a page.

**The other 89 are not lost** — every municipal office has a page here listing the services it owns, with a
link to that office's charter document. If what you need is not in the list above, start from the office.

## Find an office

Every municipal office named in the Citizen's Charter has a page: what it does for residents, which services
it owns, and the document that says so.

**Office hours are not on any of them**, because the municipality does not publish them anywhere and this
project will not guess. That is the most useful thing still missing from this site, and it says so on every
office page rather than leaving a blank.

## In an emergency

**Call 911.** It is the national emergency hotline and works from any phone.

This project has not found a municipal emergency number for Tago that it can cite — not for the disaster
office, the police, the fire station or the health unit. Rather than print a number that might be wrong, the
emergency page says where it looked and what it found. A wrong number in an emergency is worse than no number
at all.

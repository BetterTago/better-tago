# What this is

**BetterTago is an independent, volunteer-run index over the Municipality of Tago's own published
information.** It is not the official site, it is not run by the municipal government, and it is not a
replacement for either.

## The official record is the municipality's

The Municipality of Tago publishes its own website, and it is the authoritative record. **Where this site and
the official site disagree, the official site is right and this one is wrong.**

[Go to the official municipal site](https://tago.gov.ph)

## This exists because the municipality published first

Tago publishes its Citizen's Charter — 22 documents, one per office, setting out every service the municipality
provides, what it requires, and what it costs. **That is not universal, and it is the reason this project is
possible at all.** Every page here points back to one of those documents.

What was missing was not the information. It was a way to find it: a resident looking for one answer had to
know which office provides it, then find that office's document, then read a table designed for an audit.

## What this project does not do

- **It does not speak for the municipality.** No page here is an announcement, and nothing on it has been
  approved by any office.
- **It does not republish the charter's contents.** It points at the charter.
- **It does not publish a figure it cannot cite.** Where something is not known, the page says so and says what
  would be needed to know it.
- **It is not a news outlet and not a complaints channel.**

## If something here is wrong

Being wrong is ordinary and getting told is useful. There is a correction link in the footer of every page, and
a correction from the municipality goes to the front of the queue — fixed first, discussed after.

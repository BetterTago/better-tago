# How to request a statement of assets, liabilities and net worth

**This project does not publish these, and will not.** That is a settled position rather than a gap waiting to
be filled, and this page exists so that the position is stated rather than left as an unexplained absence in the
transparency register.

## Why it is not here

A statement of assets, liabilities and net worth is a personal financial disclosure about an identifiable
person. Everything else this portal restates is a document about the municipality — a budget, a fee, an office's
services. These are different in kind, and republishing them is not something a volunteer restatement project
has any business doing, however lawfully they may be requested by somebody who wants one.

The route below exists precisely so that nobody needs this project to be the one holding them.

## Where a resident asks instead

The **Office of the Ombudsman** publishes a request route and the forms that go with it. On its _Request for
SALN_ page, retrieved 2026-08-09, it names:

- **Guidelines on Public Access to Statements of Assets, Liabilities and Networth filed with the Office of the
  Ombudsman**, Memorandum Circular No. 3, Series of 2025
- **Prescribing the Fee for Requests of Statements of Assets, Liabilities and Networth**, Office Circular No. 8,
  Series of 2025
- **OMB Form 9, Series of 2025 — SALN Request Form**

## The official page

- [Request for SALN](https://www.ombudsman.gov.ph/?page_id=128060) — Office of the Ombudsman, retrieved
  2026-08-09

The Office of the Ombudsman's own page is the record. This page names what that page names and no more: the
conditions a request has to meet, what it costs and who may make one are set out there, and are not restated
here.

## What this project has not established

Whether the Municipality of Tago holds or forwards these locally, and through which office, is **not recorded
here** — no municipal source states it, checked against the official municipal site on 2026-08-09. The national
route above is the one this project can cite.

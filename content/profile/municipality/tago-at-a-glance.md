# Tago at a glance

A description of Tago as a place, built from what the municipality and cited records actually state. **What is
not known is shown as plainly as what is** — a profile with the gaps quietly left out would read as complete
and would not be.

**An aggregator site was reachable on the same sweep and was deliberately not read.** This project did not
check whether it carries these figures, because it could not use them either way: a figure that cannot be
traced to the body that published it is not one this portal can defend, and a plausible number is the one kind
of error a reader has no way to catch.

## The postal code, and why it is blank

This is the clearest example of why this portal holds rather than guesses.

**The municipality publishes a postal code on its contact page. This portal does not republish it**, because
the published value sits outside the range used for this province and appears to belong to a different part of
the country.

There are only three things this project could do, and two of them are wrong:

- Republish it — and send somebody's mail to the wrong province.
- "Correct" it to what looks right — and invent a fact, with this portal's name on it.
- **Publish nothing, say why, and confirm it against the postal service's own record.**

The third is what is happening. It stays blank until it is confirmed, and the municipality is worth telling if
their published value turns out to be a typo.

## The barangays

Five barangays are named on the municipality's tourism page — **Purisima, Cabangahan, Lindoy, Layog and Anahao
Daan** — because places listed there sit in them. 🔴 **That is not a list of the barangays of Tago**, and it
must not be read as one: it is only the barangays that happen to appear beside a tourist destination.

**No barangay list is published anywhere this project has checked**, including every page on the municipality's
sitemap, and the national record that would state one could not be retrieved. Until it is, this portal has no
barangay directory.

## What the municipality says it is working towards

Its published vision names sustainable ecological, economic and social resources supporting **ecotourism and an
agri-aqua industry**. Its tourism page names the Tago River and Nature Adventure as the focus of its tourism
development.

Those are the municipality's own stated intentions, recorded as such. **This page does not assess whether they
are being met** — that is not what a profile is for, and this project has neither the standing nor the evidence
to do it.

## Recognitions

Two are recorded, each with the announcement it came from: a **PRIME-HRM award** from the Civil Service
Commission, and a **first-place ranking for increase in real property units** covering Q4 2023 to Q4 2024. Each
is listed with what its source does and does not establish — and the PRIME-HRM entry records that the
municipality's own site describes that award in two different ways, without picking one.

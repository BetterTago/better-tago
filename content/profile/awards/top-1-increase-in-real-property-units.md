# Top 1 in highest increase in real property units, Q4 2023 to Q4 2024

**Awarding body:** Not stated outright in the announcement — see below
**What it recognises:** Ranking first for the highest increase in real property units
**Period covered:** Fourth quarter of 2023 to fourth quarter of 2024
**Status:** Reported by the municipality

## What the announcement says

The municipality published a post recording that it received recognition for ranking first in highest increase
in real property units from Q4 2023 to Q4 2024, crediting the work to the Municipal Assessor's Office, and
thanking the Provincial Assessor's Office and **BLGF Caraga** — the Bureau of Local Government Finance's
regional office — for their guidance, support and recognition.

## Why this is recorded as reported rather than confirmed

**The announcement thanks BLGF Caraga for the recognition; it does not state that BLGF Caraga conferred it**,
and no separate announcement from the awarding body was retrieved. Those are different claims, and this project
records the one the source actually supports.

That is not a doubt about whether it happened. It is the difference between _the municipality reports this_ and
_the awarding body published this_, which is exactly the distinction a reader deserves when the only source for
a recognition is the recipient.

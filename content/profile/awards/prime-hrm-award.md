# PRIME-HRM award, Civil Service Commission

**Awarding body:** Civil Service Commission
**Recognises:** Human resource management standards — see below for which award, and why that is not settled here
**Ceremony:** 20 December 2024
**Conferred:** January 2025, per the Commission's announcement

## 🔴 The municipality's own site describes this award in two different ways

This is recorded rather than resolved, because **no source this project holds reconciles them.**

**One page says Accreditation.** The Civil Service Commission's announcement, republished on the municipal
site, separates two categories. It lists five Bronze awardees — Tago is not among them — and then says:

> For Accreditation award, the following agencies, was recognized in adherence to CSC's standards in HR
> management for the two [2] core systems: Recruitment, Selection and Placement [RSP], and Performance
> Management [PM]:
>
> Municipal Government of Tago, Surigao del Sur
> Tandag City Water District

A second municipal page agrees with that reading, recording the ceremony as recognising **PRIME-HRM Maturity
Level 2** in those same two core systems.

**Another page says Bronze.** A separate municipal post is headed _PRIME-HRM BRONZE AWARD_ and opens:

> The Municipality of Tago proudly receives the PRIME-HRM Bronze Award

## Why this page does not pick one

Both pages are published by the municipality. This project restates the municipality's record; **it does not
adjudicate between two parts of it.**

Choosing the Accreditation reading would mean telling a resident the municipality is wrong about its own award
on the strength of a document this project merely read more closely. Choosing Bronze would mean ignoring an
awarding body's own published list. **Neither is this project's call**, and an award is exactly the kind of
fact where quietly picking the flattering or the unflattering reading would be equally dishonest.

**What is not in doubt:** the Civil Service Commission recognised the Municipality of Tago under PRIME-HRM,
at a ceremony on 20 December 2024, for its human resource management.

**What would settle it:** the Commission's own announcement retrieved directly from the Commission, rather than
as republished, or a correction from the municipality. Either is a change this project would be glad to make.

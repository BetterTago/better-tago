# How Tago became a municipality

The Municipality of Tago publishes its own history page. **This is a summary of what that page states, and a
link to it** — this project does no original research and adds no historical claim the municipality does not
make itself.

## The three foundings

The municipality's history page describes Tago as having been established three times, each time interrupted by
the politics of the period:

- **1865** — municipal status for the first time, under the Maura Law of the Spanish colonial administration.
  The page states it lasted three years.
- **23 August 1883** — municipal status regained, after the settlement moved from the place the page calls
  _Daan Lungsod_ (Old Town), which it describes as perennially flooded, to the place called Laguna. The page
  records a reversion to barrio status when the Philippine Revolution broke out in 1896.
- **6 November 1918** — converted from barrio to town by **Executive Order No. 41**, issued by the
  Governor-General. The first appointed municipal term began **1 January 1919**, and the page records that an
  election was held the same year.

Before 1918 Tago was part of the municipality that is now the City of Tandag; the history page describes local
leaders petitioning the Tandag municipal council, the provincial board and then Manila for separation.

## The people in this history

**This summary refers to roles rather than names**, and that is a deliberate decision rather than an omission.
The municipality's own history page names the leaders who petitioned for separation, the first appointed
municipal president and vice president, the first councillors, and the parish priest of the time. Those names
are on the official page, which is linked below, and a reader who wants them should read them there — in the
municipality's own words, in the municipality's own order, rather than re-listed by a project that added
nothing to them.

The same page closes with a sentence about which families are prominent in Tago. **That is not restated here**:
it is a claim about living people that this project cannot source beyond the assertion itself.

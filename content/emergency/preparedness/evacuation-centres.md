# Evacuation centres in Tago

**This project has not found a published list of Tago's evacuation centres.** Not on the municipality's
website, and not in the Citizen's Charter.

## Why this page has no list on it

A wrong evacuation location is the worst error this portal could make. Somebody reads it during a storm,
walks their family to a building, and the building is not a centre.

So there is no best guess here. Not a school that is probably used, not a covered court that would make
sense, not a building a neighbouring municipality uses. **A centre is listed here when a source states it is
one, and not before.**

## What is published

The municipality's disaster office publishes its Citizen's Charter, and that document describes the services
it provides to residents — disaster certification for shelter assistance, patient transfer, water delivery,
and preparedness training. **It does not list evacuation centres**, because that is not what a Citizen's
Charter is for.

## What would close this

A list from the municipal disaster office giving, for each centre: its name, the barangay it serves, where it
is, and how many people it holds. That is the same set of fields the national guidance uses, and it is
information the office holds.

Until then this page states the absence, dated, and is re-checked every 90 days.

## If you need somewhere to go now

- **Call 911.** It is the national emergency hotline and works from any phone.
- **Check the municipality's own channels** — see the **Where official advisories are posted** page. During an
  actual event, that is where an instruction about where to go would appear, and it will be newer than this
  page.
- **Your barangay hall** is the closest official point of contact and will know the arrangement for your area.

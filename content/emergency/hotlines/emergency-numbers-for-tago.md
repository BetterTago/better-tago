# Emergency numbers for Tago

**In an emergency anywhere in the Philippines, call 911.** It is the national emergency hotline and it works
from any phone.

## What this page cannot tell you yet

**This project has not found a municipal emergency number for Tago that it can cite.** Not for the municipal
disaster office, not for the police station, not for the fire station, not for rescue, and not for the rural
health unit.

That is the honest state of this page, and it is the reason the page exists rather than a reason to leave it
out. A hotline that exists but is not findable is not yet a hotline.

## Why there is no number here instead of a likely one

A wrong number in an emergency is worse than no number at all. Somebody dials it, waits, and dials again — and
that is time they did not have.

So this page will never carry:

- a number from a neighbouring municipality's directory or charter;
- a provincial office's number presented as Tago's;
- a number with no date and no source;
- a number that looks right.

**If you know a number that answers**, it is a genuinely useful thing to report, and the way to do it is in the
footer of every page on this site. It will be published as unconfirmed and labelled as such until an office or
an official document confirms it — not because a resident's word is worth less, but because that is the same
floor everything here is held to, including this project's own reading of an official PDF.

## What to do in the meantime

- **911** works from any phone, anywhere in the country, and reaches the national emergency network.
- **The municipal hall's published landline** is on the municipality's own contact page. This project has not
  rung it, so it is shown as published-but-unverified rather than as a number known to answer.
- **The municipality's own website and its social channel** are where official advisories actually appear. See
  the **Where official advisories are posted** page.

# Where official advisories are posted

If there is a storm coming, a suspended class day, or a road closed, this is where the Municipality of Tago
actually says so. **This page links those channels. It does not copy them** — this project is not a news
outlet, and a second-hand advisory is one more thing that can be out of date.

## The channels

| Channel                                   | What appears there                            | How quickly                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The municipality's Facebook page**      | Announcements, activities, and public notices | Not stated by the municipality, and this project has not measured it |
| **The municipality's website news items** | Longer announcements and event write-ups      | Not stated by the municipality                                       |

Both were checked on **9 August 2026**.

## The thing worth knowing before you need it

**The fastest official channel is on a private platform.** That is a description of how the municipality
communicates, not a criticism of it — a social page is free, it is where people already are, and standing up a
separate alerting system is a real cost for a municipality of this size.

But it does have consequences for you, and they are worth knowing on a calm day rather than a bad one:

- **A feed is ordered by the platform, not by the municipality.** A post can be published and still not appear
  near the top of what you see.
- **Reaching it can require an account**, and it depends on that company's service being up and reachable on
  your connection.
- **The municipality does not control any of that.** It can publish; it cannot guarantee the post reaches you.

So if something is happening, going to the channel directly is more reliable than waiting for it to arrive.

## What this page is not

It carries no advisory content, and it never will. **Nothing here is a substitute for the municipality's own
post**, which may be newer than this page. Where this page and an official channel disagree, the official
channel is right.

This page is re-checked every 90 days, and the date above changes when it is.

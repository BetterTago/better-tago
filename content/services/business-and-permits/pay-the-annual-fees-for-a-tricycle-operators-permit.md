# Pay the annual fees for a tricycle operator’s permit

**Who provides it:** Business Licensing and Permitting Division, Tago Municipal Hall.

## What the charter calls it

> Motorized Tricycle Operator’s Permit (MTOP) Payment of Annual Regulatory Fees for New/Renewal

That is the municipality’s own wording, reproduced exactly. It is worth knowing, because it is the wording the counter and the form will use.

**Client steps**

| Client steps | Agency action | Fees to be paid | Processing time | Person responsible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Go to the Business Permit and Licensing Office for the assessment of fees & inspection of unit and bring the requirements | 1. Inspect tricycle unit, Assess Fees and Review | None | 10 Minutes | BPLO |
| 2. Pay at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office | 2. Accept payment and Issue Official Receipt by the MTO | As per assessment | 10 Minutes | MTO - Collection Officer |
| 3.Go back to BPLO for the release of MTOP sticker | Check Official Receipt, segregate documents and release MTOP sticker | None | 5 Minutes | BPLO |

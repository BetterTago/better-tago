# Get a mayor’s permit for a business

**Who provides it:** Executive Division, Tago Municipal Hall.

## What the charter calls it

> Mayor’s Permit on Business

That is the municipality’s own wording, reproduced exactly. It is worth knowing, because it is the wording the counter and the form will use.

A certificate attesting to the tax exemption if the business is tax exempt d.

# Get a certified copy of a tax declaration

**Who provides it:** Office of the Municipal Assessor, Tago Municipal Hall.

## What the charter calls it

> Processing of Issuance of Certified True Copy of Tax Declaration

That is the municipality’s own wording, reproduced exactly. It is worth knowing, because it is the wording the counter and the form will use.

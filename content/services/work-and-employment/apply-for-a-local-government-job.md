# Apply for a local government job

**Who provides it:** Human Resource Management Division, Tago Municipal Hall.

## What the charter calls it

> Accepting Job Applications

That is the municipality’s own wording, reproduced exactly. It is worth knowing, because it is the wording the counter and the form will use.

## What the charter says

Transcribed from the municipality’s own document, in the document’s own structure and wording. Nothing below has been re-worded, re-ordered or summarised.

**Checklist of requirements**

| Checklist of requirements | Where to secure |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Application Letter | Applicant |
| 2. Resume/Personal Data Sheet | Applicant |

| 4. Latest Performance Rating (if there is any) | Applicant |

| 6. Photocopies of the trainings attended related to the job applied for (if there is any) | Applicant |
| 7. Certification from the previous employer of the experience related to the job applied for (if there is any) | Previous employer |

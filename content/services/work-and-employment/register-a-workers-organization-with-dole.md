# Register a workers’ organization with DOLE

**Who provides it:** Public Employment Office Division, Tago Municipal Hall.

## What the charter calls it

> Request for Organization/Association to be registered in DOLE

That is the municipality’s own wording, reproduced exactly. It is worth knowing, because it is the wording the counter and the form will use.

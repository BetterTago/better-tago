- → [Apply for a student summer job](/en/services/work-and-employment/apply-for-a-student-summer-job)

| Classification | Simple |
| Type of Transaction | G2SOSY – Government to Students/Out-Of-School Youth |
| Who may Avail | 15 y/o student – 30 y/o 3 rd yr. college |

**Checklist of requirements**

| Checklist of requirements | Where to secure |
| --- | --- |
| 1. SPES Application Form | — |
| 2. Photocopy of Birth Certificate or any document indicating date of birth or age (age must be 15 – 30) | — |
| 3. Photocopy of the latest Income Tax Return (ITR) of parents/legal guardian or certification issued by BIR that the parents/guardians are exempted from payment of tax or original Certificate of Indigence or original Certificate of Low Income issued by the Barangay or MSWD/CSWD where the applicant resides; and | — |
| 4. For students: any of the following, in addition to requirements no. 1 and 2: [ ] a) Photocopy of proof of average passing grade such as (1) class card or (2) Form 138 of the previous semester or year immediately preceding the application; OR [ ] b) Original copy of Certification by the School Registrar as to passing grade immediately preceding semester/year if grades are not yet available | — |

**Where to secure**, as the charter prints it — beside the list as a whole rather than against each item:

- PESO Manager Designate
- Barangay and MSWD Office
- For Out of School Youth (OSY): original copy of Certification as OSY issued by MSWD or the authorized Barangay Official where the OSY resides, in addition to requirements no. 1 and 2.

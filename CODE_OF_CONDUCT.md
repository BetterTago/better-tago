# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

BetterTago exists so that a resident of the Municipality of Tago can find a correct answer quickly. Everyone
who contributes to that — as a volunteer, a resident correcting a page, or an office answering a request — is
welcome here, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, religion, or language.

## Our standards

**Expected:**

- Assume good faith. Most errors in civic information are ordinary mistakes, not concealment.
- Credit work that has already been done. This project only exists because the municipality publishes its
  charter at all.
- Accept correction gracefully. A correction is an ordinary change, not an embarrassment, and one from the
  municipality goes to the front of the queue.
- Say what you do not know. "Requested, not yet answered" is an honest state.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information — a resident's, an official's, or a contributor's.
- Political campaigning, partisan commentary, or scorecards about officials. This is not that kind of project.
- Presenting this project as official, or implying an affiliation with the Municipal Government of Tago that
  does not exist.
- Characterising a non-response from an office as a refusal.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the project —
including any correspondence with a municipal office. **When you contact an office, identify yourself as an
independent volunteer.**

## Enforcement

Report a concern to the project maintainers through the repository's issue tracker, or privately to the project
contact address once one is published. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or
reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently bar a contributor from
participating.
